Privacy Policy — LightAway

Last updated: 29 August 2026

LightAway is made by UyuyanAslan.

We do not collect any data. LightAway does not gather, store, transmit or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

The app has no internet access

LightAway is not granted the INTERNET permission by the Android system, so it is technically incapable of sending information anywhere.

Your game data stays on your device

Your records, settings and the puzzle in progress are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them permanently. We never see them.

Links out of the app

The "More games" button opens our page on the Google Play Store in your browser or Play Store app. Anything you do there is covered by Google's privacy policy, not ours.
