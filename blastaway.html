Privacy Policy — BlastAway

Last updated: 11 August 2026

BlastAway is made by UyuyanAslan.

We do not collect any data. BlastAway does not gather, store, transmit or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

The app has no internet access

BlastAway is not granted the INTERNET permission by the Android system, so it is technically incapable of sending information anywhere.

Your game data stays on your device

Your scores, records, settings and the game in progress are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them permanently. We never see them.

Links out of the app

The "More games" button opens our page on the Google Play Store in your browser or Play Store app. Anything you do there is covered by Google's privacy policy, not ours.

Children

Because we collect nothing at all, we collect nothing from children either.
