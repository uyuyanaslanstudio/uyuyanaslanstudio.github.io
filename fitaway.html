Privacy Policy — FitAway

Last updated: 30 August 2026

FitAway is a strength-training log for Android, made by UyuyanAslan.

We do not collect any data. FitAway does not gather, store, transmit or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

The app has no internet access

FitAway is not granted the INTERNET permission by the Android system, so it is technically incapable of sending information anywhere.

Your training data stays on your device

Your exercises, your routines, every workout you have logged, the weights and repetitions in them, your notes and your settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them permanently. We never see them.

Backups you make yourself

FitAway can write your data to a JSON file, and read one back, so that a new phone or a reinstall does not cost you your history. You choose where that file goes using Android's own file picker, and it goes nowhere else: the file is written to the location you pick on your own device or your own storage account, and FitAway never uploads it, never reads it back on its own and never sees it again unless you hand it back through the same picker. Because Android's picker does the work, the app needs no storage permission at all. What happens to a file after you have saved it is between you and whatever you saved it to.

The app does not link out

FitAway contains no links, no adverts and no buttons that open anything outside itself. Nothing in the app can send you to another app, a store page or a website.

Children

FitAway is a personal training log aimed at adults. It collects nothing from anybody, of any age.
