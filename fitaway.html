Privacy Policy: FitAway

Last updated: 30 August 2026

FitAway is a training log for your exercises and routines, made by UyuyanAslan.

We do not collect any data. FitAway does not gather, store, transmit or share any personal information, from anybody, of any age. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

The app has no internet access

FitAway is not granted the INTERNET permission by the Android system, so it is technically incapable of sending information anywhere.

Everything stays on your device

The exercises you create, the routines you build, every session you have logged, the weights and repetitions in them, your notes and your settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. We never see them.

Important: there is no cloud, so please make backups

Nothing you enter is stored anywhere except on this phone. There is no account and no server holding a copy for you, so if the app is uninstalled, its data is cleared, the phone is lost or reset, or you move to a new phone, everything is gone and FitAway starts from zero: every session you logged, every exercise you created and every routine you built. Nobody can recover it for you, because nobody else ever had it.

This is the price of an app that collects nothing, and it is why the backup exists. In Settings, tap Save a local backup to write your whole log to a JSON file, and keep that file somewhere that outlives the phone. Restore a backup reads it back on the new one. Make a backup regularly, and always before you uninstall or switch phones.

You choose where the file goes using Android's own file picker, and it goes nowhere else. FitAway never uploads it, never reads it on its own and never sees it again unless you hand it back through the same picker. Because Android's picker does the work, the app needs no storage permission at all. What happens to that file afterwards is between you and wherever you put it.

The app does not link out

FitAway contains no links, no adverts and no buttons that open anything outside itself. Nothing in the app can send you to another app, a store page or a website.
