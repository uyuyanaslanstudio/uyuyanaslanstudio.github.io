Privacy Policy — SudoAway

Last updated: 28 August 2026

SudoAway is made by UyuyanAslan.

We do not collect any data. SudoAway does not gather, store, transmit or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

The app has no internet access

SudoAway is not granted the INTERNET permission by the Android system, so it is technically incapable of sending information anywhere.

Your game data stays on your device

Your puzzles in progress, candidate marks, clear counts, best times, settings and unlocks are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them permanently. We never see them.

Links out of the app

SudoAway has a "More games" button. In this release it only shows a coming-soon message: it does not open a website and makes no network request of any kind.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will appear at this address.
