SudoAway privacy policy

Effective 11 August 2026

SudoAway collects no data and has no internet access.

We collect no data

SudoAway does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or process personal information. It contains no analytics, advertising, telemetry, accounts, logins, cloud services, or tracking software.

Your game data stays on your device

Settings, active puzzles, candidate marks, progress, unlocks, and records are stored locally on your device. UyuyanAslan cannot see or recover this information. Android may remove it when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

No internet permission

The Android app does not request internet permission, so it cannot send game data or device information to UyuyanAslan or to third parties.

Links out of the app

SudoAway includes a “More games” control. In this release it only displays a coming-soon message and does not open a website or make a network request.

Changes

If SudoAway's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed version is published. The effective date above identifies the current policy.
